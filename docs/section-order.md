# Choose Your Section Order

Put your strongest relevant evidence first. Education usually leads for students and new grads; relevant work moves ahead of it as your career develops. [CareerOneStop](https://cloudfront.careeronestop.org/JobSearch/Resumes/ResumeGuide/education.aspx) uses the same distinction.

- **No internship yet:** education, projects, other experience, skills. Use the [project-first starter](../templates/no-internship-resume.tex). Keep course projects labeled as projects and separate your contribution from the team's. Other experience can be paid work, tutoring, volunteering, or a club responsibility; delete that section if it does not apply.
- **New grad with relevant work:** education, relevant experience, projects, skills. Put projects above unrelated jobs when they show more of your engineering ability.
- **Engineer with relevant work:** experience, skills, education. Start with the [one-page experienced template](../templates/experienced-one-page-resume.tex). Give recent work the most space. Keep projects only when they add evidence your jobs do not cover.
- **Changing fields:** lead with the experience or projects that match the target role. Keep entries within each section in reverse chronological order.

Certifications are optional. Move one near the top when the role requires it or it is central to the work. Otherwise, place it below stronger experience and projects, or remove it. The new-grad template's certification slot is a starting point, not a ranking rule.

Use one page until relevant content earns a second. A senior title alone does not. [MIT's resume guidance](https://capd.mit.edu/resources/career-toolkit-crafting-an-effective-resume/) allows a second page for substantial relevant experience.

The one-page experienced starter uses two roles, a short skills list, and a degree. Replace these with your actual history; two roles is not a limit. Cut repetition and less relevant detail before switching to the [two-page version](../templates/experienced-resume.tex). Neither version needs a project or certification just to fill space.

To reorder these templates, move each complete `\section{...}` block with its entries. The two-page experienced template includes an explicit `\newpage` and `\section{Experience Continued}`; remove both if you shorten it to one page. Rebuild and inspect the PDF after changing the order or page break.
